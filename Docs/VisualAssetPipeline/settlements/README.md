# Faction settlement connection studies

Three derived composition studies show how existing building concepts can share ground treatment, connection interfaces and state effects. They propose an infrastructure art layer; they do not establish new power, logistics, territory, collision or fog mechanics. Original building candidates and the [production contracts](../motion/README.md) remain the detailed design references.

## Meridian Compact

Anchor, Power Link, Array Foundry and Aegis Post share service pads, paired dark conduits, junctions and covered route crossings. Cyan inserts show active power; physical conduits remain visible when offline. Routing is illustrative and must follow the authoritative gameplay power graph. The route shown is not a new topology rule.

## Kharuun Assemblies

Memory Hearth, Growth Basin, Waystone and Listening Spine share shallow mineral roots, stratified foundation transitions and restrained amber veins. Roots disappear beneath movement crossings. This is visual continuity and local activity, not an invented electrical power mechanic.

![Kharuun base](</Volumes/Seagate Game Archive/EchoesOfTheBrokenSun/BuildArtifacts/Evidence/settlement-concepts-20260906/kharuun-basic-base-candidate.png>)

## Hollow Choir

Concordance, Chorus Loom, Interval Loom and Phase Anchor share interrupted planar ground traces and delicate coherence endpoints. The magenta Phase Anchor field is distinct from the proposed connection filaments. Filaments grant no new upkeep benefit and do not compute field membership.

![Hollow Choir base](</Volumes/Seagate Game Archive/EchoesOfTheBrokenSun/BuildArtifacts/Evidence/settlement-concepts-20260906/hollow-choir-basic-base-candidate.png>)

## Use and evidence

These are base-composition and connection-language candidates, not dimensioned layouts or replacement component turnarounds. Occluded parts and generated details are not authoritative component inventories. The exact counts, dimensions, weapon anatomy and socket contracts must come from the existing per-asset ledger. Ground/coverage marks in these sheets are not territory ownership or fog-of-war information.

The built-in image tool used retained building images as references. [generation-prompts.json](generation-prompts.json) preserves the full prompts and input hashes; [artifacts.json](artifacts.json) identifies generated outputs, corrections and inspection limits. Local Unreal, gameplay assets and unrelated active `ArtSource/` work were not modified. No rigs, runtime effects, navigation, performance or owner acceptance were tested or established.
