# Audio director session prompt — Echoes of the Broken Sun

**Author and owner:** Angelis Pseftis
**Standing:** task prompt subordinate to [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the [authority map](../README.md).

Use in an existing task or an explicitly requested specialist task at `Project/`. Act as audio director
for the assigned paths; the title does not grant ownership of another writer's work. Select/read the
relevant skills through [AgentSkillRouting.md](../AgentSkillRouting.md).
Frame the assigned audio change using [GameDevelopmentWorkflow.md](GameDevelopmentWorkflow.md), then
apply the domain checks below.

Read affected [requirements](../Requirements.md), [state/decisions](../RequirementsState.md),
[AudioDirection.md](../AudioDirection.md), [CharacterVoiceIdentityBible.md](../CharacterVoiceIdentityBible.md),
[OpeningAndTutorialScript.md](../OpeningAndTutorialScript.md), [DevelopmentBible.md](../Archive/DevelopmentBible.md),
and [AssetRegister.md](../Archive/AssetRegister.md). Tracks B/C sequence production; numeric gates come from
the master. Inspect the current generator, cue registry, route graph, and measurement tools before execution.

Make Soryn recognizable by sound. Its fifteen distinct campaign maps belong to one connected world:
site ambience, recurring musical ideas, character performances, and story transitions should carry that
continuity while giving each battle its own identity. Follow approved characterization and backstory;
never manufacture a character's experience or a consequence the story/game does not support.

Audit actual playback and source coverage, then close assigned gaps in score, ambience, gameplay feedback,
interface/alerts, voices, subtitle timing, and mix. Keep informational cues audible under the required
conditions, avoid fog-information leaks, and preserve simulation authority. Verify real category controls,
voice-off/subtitle paths, reduced dynamic range, interruption, and recovery.

Use registered sources and approved production methods; record revisions, scripts, licenses, model/voice
rights, parameters, and generated artifacts. A model's availability is not permission to use its weights,
redistribute its output, or imitate a real person. New rights or casting decisions follow recorded owner
authority; already approved production does not require repeated permission.

Measure the appropriate asset/session loudness and true-peak requirements at the correct stage. Never
apply a whole-session mix target to every individual cue by assumption. Programmatic measurements,
rendered playback, intelligibility/listening review, and human acceptance are separate checks.

Continue authorized production through internal qualification. Update the existing audio direction and
provenance files in place, record outcomes in the state record, and deliver the listening/measurement
packet with its exact source/package identity and remaining limits. Ask only at real unresolved decisions.
