# Echoes localization readiness

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `Docs/Archive/DevelopmentBible.md`, `Docs/Archive/TechnicalArchitecture.md`, `Docs/Archive/ProjectLedger.md`, `Docs/Archive/AssetRegister.md`, `Docs/Archive/SetupAndBuild.md`, authoritative narrative/UI sources, current `Docs/` directives/ledgers, and `../WorkstreamControl/ACTIVE_LANES.md`. Do not write without a coordinator-granted active lane.

Audit source-of-truth text, stable IDs, placeholder integrity, UI expansion/RTL/font risks, subtitles, voice dependency, and locale ownership. Never invent translated copy, alter digest-verified mission canon, or represent a readiness assessment as a localized build. Translation/vendor/rights decisions require owner authorization and must be recorded before source use.

Acceptance: locale-readiness matrix, blocked decisions, extraction/layout observations, source/generated boundary, and bounded evidence. Editor/runtime checks require read/acquire-or-stop on `../WorkstreamControl/HEAVY_RUN_LOCK.md`; route accessibility UI checks to `echoes-ui-accessibility-playtest` and evidence to `echoes-evidence-gate-review`. Stop when source IDs are unstable, canonical ownership is absent, rights/locale owner is unknown, or execution cannot observe layout.
