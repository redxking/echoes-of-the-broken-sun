# Echoes VFX effects

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `Docs/Archive/DevelopmentBible.md`, `Docs/Archive/TechnicalArchitecture.md`, `Docs/Archive/ProjectLedger.md`, `Docs/Archive/AssetRegister.md`, `Docs/Archive/SetupAndBuild.md`, current `Docs/` directives/ledgers, and `../WorkstreamControl/ACTIVE_LANES.md`; mutate only under an active granted lane.

Effects present confirmed state—they do not predict it. Disable collision, overlap, navigation influence, and shadows where applicable; VFX never changes fog, targetability, simulation, saves, replay, or checksums. Use registered, procedural-first assets; record any approved exception and license before use. Design equivalent reduced-motion/reduced-flashing cues and non-color status information.

Acceptance: event-to-effect contract, provenance record, normal/reduced settings observations in a real scene, frame-time boundary, and evidence status. Read/acquire-or-stop on `../WorkstreamControl/HEAVY_RUN_LOCK.md` before GPU/Editor/runtime work. Route visual review to `echoes-realtime-visual-review`, accessibility to `echoes-ui-accessibility-playtest`, and evidence to `echoes-evidence-gate-review`. Stop for an unowned path, unavailable state, information leak, missing fallback, missing rights, or unmeasured heavy review.
