World archive / Signature objective

One place.
Four visible futures.

A Future Well is a wound in reality where several possible histories remain alive at once. Armies fight around it because choosing what the Well becomes can save a city, reveal a hidden route, or erase something the world may never recover.

Art status: The Well now has original concept direction and four authored Unreal static-mesh parts with distinct Dormant, Harvest, Preserve, and Reshape presentations. The direct engine captures on this page show the current vertical-slice candidate. Final materials, detailed textures, destruction, effects, audio, animation polish, and player testing remain in development.
01 / A landmark outside the factions

Ancient.
Deliberate. Unclaimed.

The Meridian Compact builds with visible engineering. The Kharuun Assemblies cultivate mineral-organic forms. The Future Well uses neither language. Its six broken pylons hold an impossible central light while incomplete rings turn around a space that should not exist.

From the strategy camera, the broad crown identifies the objective. The center and rings show its condition. Ground paths reveal whether possibility is being drained, held, or bent into another road.

Broken crown
Six pylons establish a large, neutral silhouette that belongs to the world rather than a player.
Suspended core
A faceted piece of impossible sunlight shows whether the Well is stable, pinched, or divided.
Orbital rings
Alignment, collapse, expansion, and crossed planes communicate state through geometry.
Ground paths
Luminous routes point inward during Harvest and spread outward during Preserve or Reshape.

Harvest

Preserve

Expanded halo · Stable core · Branching paths

Preserve keeps the Well open, but the choice is not automatically gentle. Someone must hold the ground. The current prototype returns 15 Dawn every 300 ticks and provides a 1,400 cm intelligence radius while controlled.

03 / The campaign remembers

A decision is
not finished when clicked.

The opening campaign records what the player chose and carries that fact into later operations. It changes routes, priorities, and what each side believes the earlier decision meant.

M01

What the Ledger Keeps

Mara Vey recovers a lost archive, reaches the Glass Scar Well, commits one protocol, and withdraws. Destroying everything is not the objective.

The first irreversible choice is written to the campaign record: Harvest, Preserve, or Reshape.

M02

Seven Accounts of Rain

Oruun enters from the Kharuun side. The earlier choice changes the available terrain and where memory can be recovered.

The same event becomes evidence in another culture’s history, not a clean good-or-evil result.

M03

A City on Reserve

Lume Reach cannot power every district at once. The Well decision changes which continuity priority—life support, transit, or archive—comes first.

The player must reconnect real districts; victory is measured by powered systems and survival.

M04

The Unburied Road

Only one Glass Scar crossing remains available. The Kharuun must move a Waystone, establish listening infrastructure, and recover a shard along that inherited route.

Harvest opens Ash Cut, Preserve leaves the Buried Causeway, and Reshape creates the Folded Verge.

M05

Terms of Continuance

A treaty operation buckles under unresolved pressure while Meridian personnel synchronize two interfaces and protect both witnesses through a fixed survival window.

The operation consumes the same four-record history. It opens the campaign’s larger mystery, but the current mission does not identify the attacker.

M06

Names Without Births

Talar follows an erased census into one of three inherited records: the Foundry Roll, Missing Quarter, or Folded Register. He must locate the evidence before a worker can restore archive power.

Both civilian proxies must reach shelter before Talar extracts the record. The evidence proves that names are missing—not who removed them, why they vanished, or whether one hidden enemy is responsible.

M07

The Shape of Silence

The first Well decision selects Cinder Hollow, Held Hollow, or Folded Hollow. Oruun relocates a Waystone, raises listening infrastructure, positions two witnesses, and reaches their confluence.

The result records an observed correspondence between the census absence and communal-memory hollow. It does not prove cause, hidden authorship, or a Choir identity.

M08

The Shape Beside Us

The inherited choice selects one of three echo geometries. Talar, a relay worker, and two distinct witnesses make reciprocal contact repeatable through ordinary movement and construction.

The contact is actionable inside the authored operation, but it does not establish a unified or playable Hollow Choir faction.

M09

Reserve Authority

The inherited Well doctrine sets Mara’s authority site and recommends Life Support, Transit, or Archive without forcing the allocation. Exactly two districts can receive the finite reserve.

The intact unpowered district remains visible and enters the ninth record. This establishes one local decision, not wider-city recovery or civilian-survival evidence.

M10

The Choir at Lume Reach

The founding doctrine selects public contact and Mission 09’s deferred district becomes the local liability. Oruun re-roots the Waystone, raises two Listening Spines, and commits a separate Harvest, Preserve, or Reshape protocol at the Lume Well.

The tenth record preserves that new Lume decision. Mara remains off-map, the Choir is public contact rather than a playable faction, and Meridian opposition does not prove Compact-wide action.

M11

No Neutral Ledger

The founding route, Mission 09’s exact powered-district pair, and Mission 10’s Lume protocol produce 27 plans. Oruun’s Kharuun force links neutral district interfaces, attests neutral public evidence endpoints, applies only the recorded protocol, and rallies.

The eleventh record proves one bounded local coalition assembly—not mixed-faction command, a playable Choir, hidden trust, or a modeled survivor roster.

M12

The Future That Won

Oruun and an independent Kharuun verifier establish paired readback, workers complete two exact links near the inherited district inputs, and a separate Future Well accepts only the recorded Lume protocol. After a 300-tick stability hold, the scouts observe both district sites.

The twelfth record establishes local protocol activation and paired readback only—not population restoration, permanence, civilian survival, consent, trust, mixed-faction command, or playable Rhyse.

Campaign boundary: These twelve operations exist as bounded development missions with deterministic rules and automated coverage. One downstream route has complete automated evidence; the other plans do not. This is not a complete campaign, final dialogue, cinematics, broad player-tested balance, or a finished release.
04 / Readability contract

Understandable at
strategy distance.

A landmark this important cannot depend on tiny details or a single color. Its state must survive camera distance, motion settings, display variation, and the visual noise of a battle.

Shape first

Collapsed, expanded, and crossed ring layouts remain different in grayscale and at the normal RTS camera angle.

Color second

Gold, orange, cyan, and violet reinforce meaning without carrying the whole message.

Motion optional

Reduced-motion mode freezes the orbit and pulse while preserving the selected state’s geometry.
