Choose a belief.
Then make it work.

Each faction changes the questions you ask while playing. The Compact rewards connection. The Assemblies reward movement and adaptation. The Choir will challenge the idea that a battlefield has only one state.

MERIDIAN COMPACT

Build. Connect. Endure.

A precise combined-arms faction. Surveyors build the network, Lancers hold the line, Bulwark Teams create cover, and Relay Skiffs extend vision and supply.

KHARUUN ASSEMBLIES

Move. Adapt. Remember.

A mobile, terrain-integrated faction. Tenders grow the base, Riftstalkers harass, Cairnbacks screen an advance, and Resonants detect movement through vibration.

HOLLOW CHOIR

The future is not empty.

A planned later faction born from erased possibilities. It will control temporary states and competing outcomes with clear telegraphs and real counterplay.
