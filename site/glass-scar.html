World archive / First battlefield

The road broke.
Three answers crossed it.

The Glass Scar is a long impact basin beneath Lume Reach, split by black-glass ridges and the remains of an old transit line. A Future Well waits at its center. What the player does there decides which road will still exist when the campaign returns.

Environment status: The current Unreal build now uses seven original world-asset candidates for the Glass Scar terrain shelf, cliff ridge, shard clusters, three named crossings, and Matter deposits. The captures on this page come directly from the development build. They are a readable vertical-slice pass—not final terrain, textures, foliage, destruction, effects, audio, performance qualification, or a public release.
01 / The wound beneath the city

A battlefield
with a memory.

Before the Crownfall, a transit route crossed this ground. The impact dropped the basin floor, buried much of the roadway, and fused exposed stone into dark glass. The Compact later rebuilt along one rim. Kharuun memory paths grew through caverns beneath the other.

Neither side owns the center. The Well stands there like a question made physical, surrounded by broken infrastructure, exposed Matter, and routes that do not all belong to the same version of history.

Starting shelves
Opposed, readable territories give both armies space to gather, scout, and establish their first network.
Glassy ridge
A continuous east-west wound blocks a simple advance and turns each surviving crossing into a strategic commitment.
Future Well
The neutral center offers power, intelligence, or temporary terrain—and records which possibility the player chose.
Matter seams
Safer outer deposits support the opening economy. The richer center asks players to risk workers and supply routes.

03 / The inherited roads

One old decision.
Three possible routes.

Mission 04, The Unburied Road, does not ask the player to choose again. It makes Oruun’s force travel through the physical consequence of the first mission’s Well protocol.

04 / Matter and the opening economy

A resource line
is also a risk.

Matter is the practical substance of the war: mineral strata and reclaimed wreckage used for frames, bodies, and basic structures. It is different from Dawn, which comes from unrealized possibility and pays for more advanced systems.

◆

Find the seam

Surveyors and Tenders gather from pale, faceted deposits. The new Matter silhouette is narrow at the top and rooted at the base, so it does not resemble a unit or building.

◇

Carry it home

Workers have limited cargo and must return to a valid drop-off. Distance, exposure, and the survival of the route matter as much as the deposit itself.

✦

Risk the center

Outer deposits support a safer opening. Central Matter can shorten an economy, but workers near the Well are visible prizes in the most contested part of the map.

05 / The campaign thread

The ground keeps
what the ledger records.

The Glass Scar is more than the prologue map. It is the campaign’s first proof that a strategic choice can become geography, obligation, and evidence several missions later.

  1. M01

    Choose at the Well

    Mara recovers the archive, commits Harvest, Preserve, or Reshape, and withdraws. The campaign stores the result as an irreversible fact.

  2. M02–03

    See who carries the cost

    Oruun reads the choice through Kharuun memory, while Lume Reach turns it into a real priority among life support, transit, and archive continuity.

  3. M04

    Cross the road that remains

    Ash Cut, Buried Causeway, or Folded Verge becomes Oruun’s only open route to the missing shard.

  4. M05–06

    Follow the surviving record

    The same ordered history continues into treaty geometry and the census trace. It shapes what can be found without pretending it has already revealed who caused the omissions.
