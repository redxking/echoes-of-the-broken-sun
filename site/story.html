The world after
the Crownfall.

Soryn orbits a field of stellar fragments called the Crownfall. The event remembered as the breaking of the sun did not only scatter matter. It condensed unrealized causal branches into mineral-organic Dawnshards.

A shard can power a city or expose a technology that never developed. Consuming it also closes the possibility it contains.

The Glass Scar

The first operation begins outside Lume Reach, where a failing reserve threatens an evacuation and a central Future Well controls the fastest route through a fractured impact basin.

Commander Mara Vey wants enough certainty to get people out. Talar Venn wants to recover an archive convoy. Oruun-of-Seven-Stones wants to keep the Well from collapsing into a Kharuun birthing cavern. None of them has the whole truth.
