Visual archive / What it will look like

This is the target.
This is where we are.

A dark vitrified world under a shattered golden sun, where the ground recedes, the armies own the light, and every strategic fact reads before its color does. This page shows the look the Glass Scar vertical slice is aiming for, placed directly beside the accepted in-engine art-review captures of the development build.

Art status: Every image marked concept target is visual direction: not a screenshot, not the current build, and not a promise about a release date. Every image marked in-engine capture comes from the development build’s art-review fixtures with the interface hidden, as captured on 29 August 2026; surfaces, lighting, and exposure have changed in source since, and no newer public capture has been accepted. Statements about what exists in source reflect the project ledger as of 4 September 2026. There is no public playable release. No Hollow Choir concept art exists yet; first-pass Choir meshes are in the development build, and no public capture of them has been accepted.
01 / The composed target

One frame.
Every layer where it belongs.

Read it the way the finished game is meant to be read: matte charcoal ground and gold fracture veins are meant to recede, the Future Well is meant to orient the fight without shouting, and Meridian and Kharuun silhouettes are meant to carry the color while the Broken Sun enters from above. This is a low, cinematic framing, closer to a title shot than to the strategy camera, and no interface is drawn.

Read last

Terrain recedes

The ground is meant to carry the lowest saturation and no glow that competes with an actor. Veins may be beautiful only in ways that step back.

Read second

Landmarks orient

The Well and the causeway are meant to hold their light below any unit’s claim. Landmarks point; they do not compete.

Read first

Actors own the light

Compact machines on the left, Assemblies warforms on the right. Faction is meant to read from shape before cyan or amber does.

Felt, not stared at

The Crownfall duality

Gold key against indigo fill is the lighting signature. From the strategy camera the sun is felt through warm light and vein glow, not seen directly.

Not in frame: Surveyors and Tenders, every structure, the Hollow Choir, the interface, fog of war, ownership marks, order and selection feedback, and telegraphs. The two brightest layers of the finished frame, transient feedback and interface, are absent, which is one more reason this is not a screenshot. The render composes surfaces, light, and silhouettes; it is not the map layout, and its Well core is a direction rather than the settled design.

02 / The build as last captured

The distance
between the two.

The development build already places the two starting shelves, the east–west ridge, the three crossings, the Matter seams, and the central Well as authored meshes, and its first surface families, motion families, and combat effects exist in source. What the target adds is what the build has not yet earned a public capture for: production surfaces and wear, atmosphere, qualified lighting, and a composed frame that passes the readability bar at combat speed. The gap is the work, and the Updates page tracks it.

Left: concept direction for surfaces, lighting, and silhouettes. Right: the accepted 29 August 2026 art-review capture from the development build, interface hidden. The shelves, ridge, three crossings, and the Well’s position come from the deterministic map shown on the right; the render is not the map. Neither image is a screenshot of play.

03 / The five notes

A palette you can
name from across the map.

Every surface, effect, and interface element in the finished game must belong to one of five notes, plus one cool complement for the sky. A color that cannot name its note does not ship.

Terrain and mass

Charcoal

Terrain body, structure mass, and interface panel ground. Never lifted to grey by fill light.

Pale ceramic

Compact-built surfaces and interface type, with wear at edges and load points rather than uniform grime.

website swatch #ecebe6
The warm accent

Broken-sun amber

Fracture veins, Kharuun mineral identity, the key light, and alerts.

website swatch #f0b45a
Possibility bleed

Magenta-fracture

Crownfall phenomena, the bleed of erased futures, and the Hollow Choir’s identity.

website swatch #b3417a
Matter and confirmation

Cyan

Matter deposits, Meridian engineered systems, and interface confirmation.

website swatch #5fd8e8
The sky’s cool half

Indigo

Fill light and shadow temperature, not a surface color. Gold against indigo is the Crownfall duality.

05 / The factions in form

Shape first.
Color second.

A silhouette that needs its accent color to say which faction it is has failed the check. These are the registered concept sheets for the two factions that have them, with the cues the finished silhouettes are meant to carry.

How the Compact reads

Engineered load paths

  • Orthogonal rails, plates, and exposed connections
  • Visible repair states: equipment kept working by its users
  • Cyan accents on ivory ceramic and graphite
  • Bracketed rectangular ownership marks, described here rather than drawn
How the Assemblies read

Grown mineral structure

  • Facets, cones, nodules, and layered strata
  • Inhabited and maintained, never primitive
  • Amber accents per the art direction; this sheet and the composed target add limited violet crystal detail that the art direction does not assign, and the in-engine Kharuun accent is amber
  • Paired faceted ownership marks, described here rather than drawn
Hollow Choir

No concept sheet yet

The Choir does not appear in the composed target and has no registered concept sheet. Its intended form language is repeated luminous edges with deliberate contradiction: offset duplicates, spans with two valid shadows, forms that stay real only while coherence holds, and a magenta identity. First-pass Choir meshes exist in the development build; no public capture of them has been accepted. The Choir will be shown here only when a registered sheet or an accepted capture exists.

06 / What has to happen between the two

When a capture earns it,
the label changes.

Each item below is work, not a promise. When the build meets the composed-frame bar for a subject, a capture from the running game replaces the concept target here, and the Updates page says so.

Surfaces

Production surfaces

Deterministic texture families exist in source for the ground, civic ceramic, Matter deposits, and each faction. Per-family composition review, route-kit binding, route-specific wear, and surface response at combat zoom are still ahead.

Motion

Animation you can see

Code-driven motion families exist in source for walkers, hover units, workers, structures, and landmarks. No public capture of them has been accepted, so none is shown here.

Effects

Effects and telegraphs

Weapon, impact, gather, construction, and Reshape effects exist in source as presentation-only families. They wait for an accepted capture before they appear on this site.

Atmosphere

Lighting qualification

One authored light rig exists for the Glass Scar. Composed frames must pass a measured exposure and readability bar before their captures replace targets.

People

Characters and cinematics

No character art or in-engine cinematic has been accepted yet. The people of Soryn are described on the Characters page in words.

Third faction

Hollow Choir presentation

Meshes exist in the development build; a registered concept sheet and an accepted public capture do not.
