Building the
first future carefully.

This page separates the game that is being designed from the software and presentation that exist today. The foundation is real development work. It is not yet a finished game or a public release.

Plain answer: You cannot download and play a polished version of Echoes of the Broken Sun today. Current builds use labeled squares, circles, engine primitives, and other temporary geometry. Final character art, unit models, terrain, animation, effects, and audio have not been produced.
Current development state

What exists
under the placeholders.

The project is moving from a systems prototype toward a Glass Scar vertical slice. The exact boundaries matter: a tested rule or bounded route does not establish final balance, broad usability, production art, or release readiness.

Implemented prototype

Two feature-gated operations

A Glass Scar skirmish and the Meridian-locked What the Ledger Keeps prologue exist in development form, with operation-specific briefing, objectives, results, restart, and isolated quick saves.

Implemented prototype

Two playable faction foundations

Meridian and Kharuun each have a worker, line or skirmish unit, heavy screen, scout, headquarters, supply structure, production structure, utility or defense structure, and a two-step research path.

Implemented prototype

A Future Well decision with memory

The first successful prologue choice—Harvest, Preserve, or Reshape—can be written as a durable campaign consequence. Same-choice replay is idempotent, while a different replay cannot silently rewrite the first recorded result.

Current website

A readable player archive

The public site now separates world lore, character arcs, faction beliefs, every current unit and building, the full planned campaign, and honest development status into dedicated pages.

What is not here yet
Presentation

Finished game graphics

Not yet. Existing geometry is functional and labeled for development. It should not be mistaken for the intended final visual quality.

Availability

Public playable release

Not yet. The GitHub Pages site explains the concept and progress; it does not provide a release build or promise a launch date.

Campaign

Missions 2–15

Planned in the authoritative campaign outline, but not implemented as playable operations. Broad choice persistence and final dialogue or cinematics remain ahead.

Faction

Playable Hollow Choir

The culture, rules, and narrative role are designed. A complete unit catalog and playable implementation have not been delivered.

Modes

Multiplayer and replay UI

Planned design areas, not available features. They are not shown as playable options on this website.

Release work

Final tuning and distribution

Balance qualification, production performance, signing, notarization, clean-machine compatibility, and distribution readiness remain open.

Next horizons

From working rules
to a readable world.

The immediate creative goal is not more placeholder spectacle. It is a coherent vertical slice in which players can recognize faction, role, threat, terrain, orders, and Well transformation at combat speed.

Visual identity

Production silhouettes

Develop final unit and structure forms that communicate ownership and tactical role without relying on color alone.

World building

The complete Glass Scar

Build final terrain direction, readable routes, Future Well transformations, ecology, atmosphere, and destruction that communicates functional loss.

Player experience

Polished prologue flow

Turn the bounded first-operation route into a coherent experience with final writing, presentation, accessibility work, sound, and broad usability testing.

Longer arc

Campaign systems

Expand one durable decision into campaign slots, branching consequences, later-mission consumption, relationships, civilian state, and multiple endings.
