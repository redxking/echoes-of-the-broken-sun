Development archive / Source snapshot August 30, 2026

Building the
first future carefully.

This page separates the game that is being designed from the software and presentation that exist today. The foundation is real development work. It is not yet a finished game or a public release.

Plain answer: You cannot download and play a polished version of Echoes of the Broken Sun today. The current Unreal source integrates an original first-pass mesh for every Meridian, Kharuun, and Hollow Choir unit and structure, a four-part Future Well, and seven authored Glass Scar world assets. These are vertical-slice art candidates, not finished production models; character art, production terrain, animation, detailed textures, destruction, effects, and audio remain incomplete.
Current development state

What exists
under the placeholders.

The project is moving from a systems prototype toward a Glass Scar vertical slice. The exact boundaries matter: a tested rule or bounded route does not establish final balance, broad usability, production art, or release readiness.

Implemented prototype

Fifteen feature-gated operations

The Glass Scar skirmish and fourteen linked campaign missions now exist in development form: What the Ledger Keeps, Seven Accounts of Rain, A City on Reserve, The Unburied Road, Terms of Continuance, Names Without Births, The Shape of Silence, The Shape Beside Us, Reserve Authority, The Choir at Lume Reach, No Neutral Ledger, The Future That Won, Assembly of the Missing, and Several Voices, One Command. Each has its own operation state, objective flow, result, replay path, and isolated quick save.

Implemented prototype

Three playable faction foundations

Meridian, Kharuun, and Hollow Choir each have a worker, line or skirmish unit, heavy or recovery unit, scout or support unit, headquarters, supply structure, production structure, utility structure, and a two-step research path. These are prototype foundations, not a broad balance result.

Art candidate

Twenty-four authored roster meshes

Every current Meridian, Kharuun, and Hollow Choir unit and structure now loads a distinct original static mesh in Unreal. Each asset includes two LODs, four faction-aware material zones, and visibility selection collision. The public Arsenal distinguishes live captures, source-integrated candidates, and visual targets.

Art candidate

Four-state Future Well landmark

The world’s signature objective now uses authored base, orbit, core, and ground-glyph meshes. Dormant, Harvest, Preserve, and Reshape remain readable through different geometry when color or motion is reduced. The Future Well archive pairs current Metal captures with the clearly labeled concept target and exact prototype rules.

Art candidate

Seven authored Glass Scar world assets

The current environment pass replaces visible terrain and resource primitives with original shelf, ridge, shard, Ash Cut, Buried Causeway, Folded Verge, and Matter-deposit meshes. Each has two LODs and four material zones. The world archive separates the polished concept target from accepted current Unreal captures.

Implemented prototype

Decisions carried through fourteen records

The prologue’s Harvest, Preserve, or Reshape choice is written as a durable fact. Later missions consume it as migration terrain, district priority, an unburied road, treaty geometry, a census trace, listening and contact geometry, and a finite reserve recommendation. Mission 10 records a separate Lume protocol; Missions 11–13 carry the three established choice axes through bounded public coalition, readback, linkage, and observation work. Mission 14 inherits the exact thirteen-record ledger and records one Hollow Choir command-crisis hold without deciding the Choir’s final fate. All fourteen ordered records must remain consistent; replay cannot silently rewrite an earlier result.

What is not here yet
Presentation

Finished game graphics

Not yet. The roster, Future Well, and first Glass Scar world assets are functional and visible in-engine, but they remain static vertical-slice candidates without final textures, animation, destruction, effects, or full lighting qualification.

Availability

Public playable release

Not yet. The GitHub Pages site explains the concept and progress; it does not provide a release build or promise a launch date.

Campaign

Mission 15 and full routes

The final operation remains planned. Alternate complete routes, broad branch coverage, campaign-slot UX, final dialogue, and cinematics remain ahead.

Faction

Finished Hollow Choir

The source roster, technologies, bounded mechanics, skirmish choice, and Mission 14 command perspective exist. Broad matchups, final presentation, full usability, and the Choir’s ending remain undelivered.

Modes

Multiplayer and replay UI

Planned design areas, not available features. They are not shown as playable options on this website.

Release work

Final tuning and distribution

Balance qualification, production performance, signing, notarization, clean-machine compatibility, and distribution readiness remain open.

Next horizons

From working rules
to a readable world.

The immediate creative goal is not more placeholder spectacle. It is a coherent vertical slice in which players can recognize faction, role, threat, terrain, orders, and Well transformation at combat speed.

Visual identity

Production silhouettes

Refine the new authored mesh set through camera-distance recognition tests, skeletal articulation, damage states, texture detail, and non-color faction cues.

World building

The complete Glass Scar

Refine the new terrain, route, Matter, and Future Well candidates into one production environment with ecology, atmosphere, destruction, and tested combat-distance readability.

Player experience

Polished campaign arc

Turn the fourteen bounded campaign operations into a coherent experience with final writing, presentation, accessibility work, sound, and broad usability testing.

Longer arc

Campaign systems

Extend the fourteen-record chain through Mission 15, campaign slots, broader consequences, relationships, civilian state, and multiple endings.
