A world carved by possibility.
Fifteen battlefields of Soryn.

From the vitrified fractures of the Glass Scar to the blinding corona of the Solar Fall Dais, every battlefield on Soryn carries a distinct geological history, environmental hazards, tactical choke points, and soundscapes. Explore the continental arteries and operational sectors.

01 / Continental Cartography

The theater of war.
One fractured continent.

Soryn’s habitable landmass is bound together by pre-Crownfall transit spans, deep tectonic fissures, and the perpetual amber glare of the Broken Sun. Ground forces cannot travel without reckoning with the scars left by lost futures.

Artery 01

The Glass Highway

Extends south from the ark-city of Lume Reach through the vitrified Glass Scar into the outer lowlands. The primary logistics conduit for Meridian power line expansions and convoy evacuations.

Artery 02

The Subterranean Conduits

Ancient transit caverns and crystal geode tunnels plunging miles below the surface. Controlled by Kharuun Assemblies migration paths connecting the birthing caverns to deep mineral deposits.

Artery 03

The Sheared Foundry Spine

Heavy industrial foundations connecting Ark-City Sector 9 and the Census Vaults. Towering monolithic silos overlook chasms where entire city districts were cleaved away during the Crownfall.

Artery 04

The Solar Ascent

A forbidding basalt escarpment climbing directly toward the sub-solar ground zero. Platforms and bridges suspend over the void, leading to the Solar Fall Dais directly beneath the broken star.

05 / Skirmish Theaters

Symmetric proving grounds.
Competitive battlefield setups.

In addition to the campaign operations, six standalone skirmish theaters provide balanced, highly competitive arenas for custom player-versus-AI and multiplayer engagements.

Skirmish 01 · 1v1

Twin Wells

Two contested Future Wells occupy mirrored high-ground plateaus separated by a deep central ravine. Players must divide forces between defending their primary Well and denying the enemy’s second expansion.

Skirmish 02 · 1v1 / 2v2

Crucible Ridge

A broad central mesa flanked by narrow flanking defiles. Rich Matter deposits sit exposed on the low ground, tempting early harvesters into vulnerable crossfire pockets.

Skirmish 03 · 2v2 / 4-Player FFA

Sunken Foundry

A vast semi-subterranean industrial complex with four corner spawn zones. Low-lying fog of war obscures movement through the central slag canals, enabling surprise ambushes.

Skirmish 04 · 1v1

Frostshard Expanse

A high-altitude glacial steppe where brittle Dawnshard drifts create destructible path blockers. Breaking shard walls can open sudden attack lanes directly into enemy expansions.

Skirmish 05 · 3v3 / 6-Player

Shattered Causeway

Three massive parallel transit causeways spanning an abyssal canyon. Forces must coordinate across all three spans simultaneously, as losing any bridge allows a devastating flanking encirclement.

Skirmish 06 · 1v1 Tournament

Choir Sanctum

A strictly symmetrical geometric arena centered around a high-yield neutral Well. Acoustic pillars allow units with stealth or vibration tracking to maneuver unseen by standard line-of-sight.
